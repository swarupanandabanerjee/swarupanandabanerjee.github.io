About me

PhD research scholar in applied mathematics at IIT Kharagpur, working on the analysis of PDE models for biological and porous media.

Biography

I am a PhD research scholar in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, where I have been working since July 2024 under the supervision of Prof. Hari Shankar Mahato and Prof. G. P. Raja Sekhar.

My research focuses on mathematical modeling and applied analysis of complex biological and porous media. I work on the well-posedness and homogenization of partial differential equation (PDE) models, with particular emphasis on biphasic mixture and visco-morphoelastic models in biological and tissue-engineering systems.

Before starting my PhD, I was a Junior Research Fellow at IIT Ropar, working on multi-scale analysis and computation of optimal control problems, and at IIT Bhilai, working on variational methods for non-local problems. I received my M.Sc. in Mathematics from Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda Educational and Research Institute, Belur, and my B.Sc. in Mathematics from Bankura Christian College.

Alongside research, I am a teaching assistant for NPTEL and IIT Kharagpur mathematics courses and a Subject Matter Expert for the GATE–NPTEL initiative.
