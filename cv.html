Short bio

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Mathematics at IIT Kharagpur, working under the supervision of Prof. Hari Shankar Mahato and Prof. G P Raja Sekhar. My research focuses on mathematical modeling and applied analysis of complex biological and porous media. I work with homogenization theory and PDEs, with particular emphasis on biphasic mixture and morpho-viscoelastic models in biological and tissue-engineering systems.
